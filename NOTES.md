# Notes

## On the fork

This started as a personal tweak of
[mdomlop/retrosmart-x11-cursors](https://github.com/mdomlop/retrosmart-x11-cursors)
and turned into a rebuild of the packaging/build pipeline: new sizes, color
scheme handling, theme variants. The cursor designs are still Manuel's —
this fork adapts them, it doesn't reinvent them.

## On AI assistance

Build scripts and docs were put together with AI help. The cursor art in
`src/base/` was hand-drawn/hand-edited, not AI-generated.

## On the wording in the README

"Touch-ups" and "refinements," not "fixes." The point isn't that the
original art had problems — any small edits happened while adapting the
cursors to new sizes and hotspots, not because the originals needed
correcting.
